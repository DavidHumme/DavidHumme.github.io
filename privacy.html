Privacy Policy

Wanderways: Hearthstead ("the game"), made by David Humme ("we").
Last updated: 3 November 2026.

We don't run our own servers and we don't collect your personal data ourselves. The game uses a few Google services, which handle some data as described below.

What stays on your device

Your progress (your village, coins, heroes and settings) is saved on your device. We can't see it. Uninstalling the game deletes it, unless you use cloud saves (below).

Cloud saves (Google Play Games)

If you sign in with Google Play Games, a copy of your game save is stored in your Google account, so you can continue on another device. This uses your Play Games player profile. Google handles this data under the Google Privacy Policy. You can delete saved games in the Google Play Games app or in your Google account settings.

Purchases (Google Play)

In-app purchases are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details. The game only learns which item was bought, so it can give it to you.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The game offers optional ads that you choose to watch for a reward. These ads are provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address (approximate location), and how you interact with the ads, to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. Ads are limited to family-friendly content.

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before ads are personalized. You can change your choice at any time in the game under Menu → Settings → Privacy choices (ads). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal data from them.

Your rights

Because we don't hold your personal data, there is nothing for us to export or delete. For data held by Google, use your Google account settings. For questions about this policy, contact us at hearthstead.games@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
