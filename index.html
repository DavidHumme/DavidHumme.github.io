Wanderways

Hearthstead

Gerald the goose ate the Great Compass's needle. Then he sneezed. Twelve times.

Now the Four Roads wander, Hearthstead drifts, and the twelve grumpiest creatures out there each hold a shard. You were late to the village meeting, which in Hearthstead counts as volunteering.

Wanderways: Hearthstead is a cozy idle runner. Run the roads (or let them run while you nap), bonk critters, befriend bosses who become your pen pals, rebuild and rearrange your own village, grow surprises in the Seed Patch, and find out why the goose really did it.
